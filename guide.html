STEP BY STEP

Download a PDF

01 / OPEN THE LIBRARY

Choose your section

On the homepage, select Mid-1, Mid-2 or Semester. For general files, choose Other Important Documents.

02 / PICK A SUBJECT

Find your material

Select your subject and browse the available materials. Other Important Documents opens general files directly.

03 / SAVE YOUR FILE

Tap Download ↓

Use Preview to read supported files, Download to save a copy, or Share to send the material link.

STAY IN THE LOOP

Bhaai Tweet & Notes

From the homepage, open the Bhaai Tweet & Notes card or use Notes in the header. Read the latest messages and PDF upload updates. If an update includes a material link, tap it to open that material.
